Cross-Dataset Stable PE Feature Selection (Research Methodology)

The Problem: Models trained on a single year's dataset (e.g. EMBER 2018) rapidly decay over time because they overfit to transient temporal artifacts (e.g., year-specific import table hashes or compiler versions) that adversaries modify or deprecate.

Multi-Year Consensus: Our algorithm measures feature stability across independent temporal distributions (EMBER 2017 v2 and EMBER 2018). Only features that consistently retain high discriminative power across both years are selected into the Top-K Consensus Subset, recovering +295 missed threats (false negatives) under temporal distribution shift on the 2020 BODMAS benchmark.
